How to receive a file
  • Ask the TX Operator to share the TX Client window on the call, as large as possible.
  • Click Capture a window and pick the window or screen region showing the Slides.
  • Watch the progress bar. When it stalls, copy the Gap Report and send it to the TX Operator over chat.
  • When the transfer completes and the digest verifies, save the file.
  • Red banners are incoherence: something is wrong beyond ordinary channel loss. Don't trust the transfer until it's resolved.
